### 🔬 About me

I'm a Ph.D. student at the School of Optometry, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My research explores medical agents, multimodal ophthalmic AI, and privacy-preserving federated learning.

### ✨ Selected projects

- 🩺 **[FedEYE](https://github.com/yyyanbj/FedEYE)** — An end-to-end federated learning platform for ophthalmology.
- 🧠 **[Buffalo](https://github.com/yyyanbj/Buffalo)** — Biomedical vision-language understanding with federated foundation models.
- 📄 **[arxiv-daily](https://github.com/yyyanbj/arxiv-daily)** — Automatically collects recent arXiv papers in selected fields.
- 🗂️ **[EMR Extractor](https://github.com/yyyanbj/emr-extractor)** — Extracts and structures scanned medical records.
